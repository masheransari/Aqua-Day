2.Information handled by AquaDay

AquaDay is designed as an offline-first water-intake tracker. You do not need to create an account or sign in to log water, change your goal, view history or use local reminders. The App does not ask for your name, phone number, contacts, precise location, camera access or medical records for these features.

There is an important distinction between water-tracking information held locally and technical information processed by advertising or other enabled third-party SDKs.

3.Your water log and app settings

AquaDay records the water amounts and times that you enter manually, your personal daily goal, and preferences such as units, theme and reminder hours. These details support your daily progress, history, and optional reminders. You can correct or delete individual water entries in the App.

Daily progress is calculated using your recorded entries and goal. A new local day starts a new daily total; older entries remain in History until deleted. The App does not diagnose hydration levels, calculate medical fluid requirements, or request medical records.

The planned Android implementation uses local storage such as Room and DataStore rather than an AquaDay account or developer-operated synchronisation server. AquaDay does not intentionally include your water log in AdMob ad requests.

The availability of Android operating-system backup or device-transfer copies depends on the released App's backup configuration and the device. Removing a water entry in AquaDay does not necessarily remove independent device backups that may exist.

Your hydration target is one you choose; it is not a medical recommendation. Please read the Terms and Conditions for health-related limitations.

4.Advertising with Google AdMob

AquaDay is free to use and uses Google AdMob to display banner advertisements on eligible app screens. The App is designed not to display advertisements on onboarding or legal-document pages. An interstitial Ad Unit ID may exist in the App's configuration, but full-screen interstitial ads are disabled in the current planned release.

When ads are requested, the Google Mobile Ads SDK may automatically collect and share technical information, including IP address (which can indicate approximate location), app and device identifiers including an advertising ID where available, app interactions, advertising interactions, and diagnostic or performance information. Google uses these for advertising, measurement, analytics and protection against invalid traffic or fraud.

Ads may be personalised or non-personalised depending on applicable law, settings and choices. Water logs, individual intake amounts and reminder times are not used by AquaDay to target advertisements.

You can manage advertising identifiers through your Android device settings and some advertising preferences through Google My Ad Center.

Learn more about Google's practices: Google partner sites and apps, Advertising technologies, and Mobile Ads SDK data disclosure.

5.Advertising consent and privacy options

AquaDay integrates Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) for advertising privacy messages and choices where required. UMP may process technical information to determine which message applies and to record your choices.

  • Where required, you may see a consent or privacy notice before ads are requested.
  • The App should request ads only when Google's SDK indicates that advertising requests are permitted.
  • When Google requires a privacy-options entry point for your region, you can review your choices using Settings → Advertising privacy choices.
  • You can continue using the core water-tracking and reminder features even if advertising consent is not granted.

More details: Google UMP documentation and Google Privacy Policy.

6.Diagnostics and optional Firebase services

Reports may contain information provided by the app or included in error diagnostics. The developer must verify that hydration data is not attached to reports before making that claim.

If Firebase Analytics is enabled: app events and usage information may be collected. If it is present with analytics collection disabled, that setting must be verified in the released build.

9.Water reminders

Reminders are optional. You can choose an active time window and a reminder interval. The app schedules local notifications through Android rather than sending reminder data to an AquaDay-operated server.

Reminders are approximate. Android power management, notification permissions, device restarts, force-stop, manufacturer settings, and other factors may delay or prevent delivery. You may turn reminders off in AquaDay or in Android's notification settings. The app is not an emergency-alert system.

10.Retention and deletion

  • Water entries and preferences: Local records remain until you edit/delete them, clear the app's storage, or uninstall the App. Clearing storage removes the App's local data. Device backups, if enabled or previously created, may be handled separately by Android or your device provider.
  • Advertising and consent-related data: Google controls retention under its own policies. Android settings and Google's privacy controls provide options for advertising identifiers and choices.
  • Optional diagnostic data: If a diagnostic SDK is enabled, its provider's retention terms apply and must be reflected in the published policy.

AquaDay does not provide user accounts, so there is no AquaDay account to delete. If you have a privacy inquiry, contact the publisher below.

11.Data security

Local water logs are designed to be held in Android app-private storage, which provides operating-system isolation from ordinary access by other apps. The App does not claim that local hydration records are separately encrypted beyond the protections supplied by the device and operating system.

Internet-connected services such as Google AdMob use their own security controls. No storage or transmission method is completely risk-free. Protecting access to your device also helps protect information visible inside AquaDay.

12.Children's privacy

AquaDay is intended as a general wellness tool, not a child-directed application. It does not ask users to register or provide personal identity details for hydration tracking. Third-party advertising services may process technical device information as described above.

If you are a parent or guardian with a privacy concern, please contact us at asheransar@gmail.com.

13.International processing and privacy rights

Google and any other enabled third-party providers may process certain technical information in countries other than yours, subject to their applicable safeguards and policies.

Depending on your location, privacy laws may give you rights to access, correct, delete or restrict certain processing, or to withdraw consent. You can manage water entries within the App and use advertising privacy controls where offered. For requests concerning information processed by the publisher, use the contact details below; for Google-held data, Google's relevant privacy controls may also apply.
