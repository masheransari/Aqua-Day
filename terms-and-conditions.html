2.Purpose of the App

AquaDay is a personal water-intake diary and reminder app. Subject to device capabilities and the installed version, it lets you:

  • log a drink quickly or enter a custom amount, and edit or delete water entries;
  • choose a daily goal and view daily progress, recent activity and history;
  • configure optional reminder notifications with active hours and a reminder interval;
  • set display preferences, including supported measurement units and appearance options.

AquaDay does not require a login or developer-operated cloud account. Its water-tracking functions are designed to work without an internet connection; advertisements and hosted legal pages require connectivity.

3.Medical disclaimer

AquaDay does not provide medical advice. The App is a general wellness and habit-tracking tool. It is not a medical device and is not intended to diagnose, treat, cure, monitor or prevent any disease or condition.

  • Any example or default goal displayed by AquaDay is a configurable starting value, not a recommendation for your individual fluid needs.

  • Fluid needs vary from person to person. Some medical conditions (for example certain heart or kidney conditions) and some medications require limiting or carefully managing fluid intake. Drinking too much water can also be harmful.

  • Do not ignore professional medical advice or delay seeking it because of anything shown in the App. If you think you have a medical emergency, contact your local emergency services immediately.

4.Tracking and reminder limitations

  • Manual accuracy: AquaDay only knows about drinks that you enter. Totals, progress indicators, trends and previous-day history reflect those entries.
  • Daily totals: A new local calendar day starts a new daily total. Older history is not automatically deleted at midnight. Goal percentages may exceed 100% without implying that additional drinking is appropriate.
  • Display units: Water amounts may be entered or displayed in millilitres or fluid ounces; unit conversion can involve rounding.
  • Reminders: Notifications are approximate and may be delayed or missed due to Android restrictions, permission choices, battery settings, device shutdown, or force-stop.
  • Local records: There is no AquaDay-operated cloud sync. Your data may be lost if you clear the App's data, uninstall it, or replace/lose your phone. Any Android device backups depend on applicable device and build settings.

AquaDay must not be relied upon as the sole method of tracking medically important fluid intake or providing time-critical alerts.
